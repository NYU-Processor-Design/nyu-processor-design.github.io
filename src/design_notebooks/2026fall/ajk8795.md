## week 2: 9/22-9/28

### work completed
- read the excerpt on the architecture of a single cycle MIPS processor from Patterson and Hennessy
- compared the MIPS implementation to what I know about the RV32 ISA

### notes from reading
- the control block is often the clock speed bottleneck, which is one of the central motivations for pipelining in processor design
- sacrificing some space in the instruction is often worthwhile to make the placement of signal consistent or at least similar for many instructions
- The ISA shapes the architecture - it is almost like a datapath spec
- where possible, the decoding of instructions is hierarchical in a single-cycle MIPS processor, which helps simplify the control logic a lot
